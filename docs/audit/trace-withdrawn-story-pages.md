# Withdrawn and superseded story pages (PR #10)

**Branch:** `claude/trace-withdrawn-story-pages`, from `main` at `c41b6b1`. This is a Pages-only change: no Worker deploy, migration or D1 change.

## Problem

`/stories/[slug]` served only stories passing the published-only rule, so the page's withdrawn and superseded banners never rendered. Production story 381 returned **404** after its withdrawal on 2026-10-25, contrary to the corrections-policy text on the page. The remaining cleanup withdrawals (384–388) are paused until this ships.

## Behaviour after this change

| Story | Public page | Listings, homepage, feed, RSS, sitemap | Indexing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Published | Unchanged | Included (unchanged) | Indexable |
| Withdrawn after publication | **200** at the original URL, with a withdrawal notice | Excluded | `noindex` |
| Superseded after publication | **200**, with a superseded notice; links the replacement only when a reviewed `supersedes` relationship names a currently published story | Excluded | `noindex` |
| Archived from draft or review, never published | 404 | Excluded | – |
| `withdrawn`/`superseded` with incomplete publication metadata (no slug, `published_at`, `reviewed_by` or `reviewed_at`) | 404 | Excluded | – |

**What a retired page shows:**
- **Notice:** a `role="note"` section headed "This story has been withdrawn" or "This story has been superseded". It says the original text, analysis, source links and evidence rating are kept for transparency and historical reference, and are **not current TRACE reporting or verified information**. It names no single cause, because withdrawals have different causes.
- **Historical labelling:** the evidence badge and rating explanation are labelled "Evidence rating at publication (historical, not a current assessment)", and the summary and analysis headings read "(as published)".
- **Sources and corrections:** the story's sources and published corrections stay visible.
- **Unchanged and gated:** evidence and related-story enrichment keep the published-only rule, so they show nothing for a retired story. That keeps retired content out of other pages' related lists.
- **The sitemap** is generated by `@astrojs/sitemap` from static routes and has never listed SSR story pages.

## Withdrawal reasons (limitation)

TRACE stores **no structured withdrawal reason**. `/admin/withdraw-story` appends the operator's reason to `editorial_analysis` as `[Withdrawn: …]` prose, which the page already shows in "TRACE Analysis (as published)". The page does not parse reasons out of prose, and no migration was added for this. If a prominent structured reason is wanted, it needs a `withdrawal_reason` column (or a correction record written by the withdraw route) as a separate change.

**Supersession, by contrast, is structured:** reviewed `story_relationships` rows with `relationship = 'supersedes'` (source supersedes target), looked up through the existing `idx_story_relationships_target` index, `LIMIT 1`.
